About This Course

Sir Malik Ghulam Abbas's advanced learning path explores inflation data, employment releases, economic growth, central-bank decisions, bond yields, the US Dollar Index and risk sentiment. Students study these market drivers as interconnected factors and learn to build a reasoned fundamental outlook.
