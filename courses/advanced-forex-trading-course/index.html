About This Course

Sir Sajid Khan Ghori covers professional habits, session timing, currency strength, correlation analysis, order-flow concepts, sentiment, trade planning and position management. The aim is to help students build and review a decision-making process, not copy signals without context.

Students are encouraged to practise on a demo account and apply consistent risk controls. Current course fees, batch access and payment instructions are available through the regular PipSePaisa enrollment system.
