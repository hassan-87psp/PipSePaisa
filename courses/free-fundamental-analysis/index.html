About This Course

Sir Malik Ghulam Abbas explains how to navigate the economic calendar, understand policy statements, and interpret FOMC decisions. The focus is on market expectations and context rather than treating one positive or negative data point as a guaranteed trading direction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is fundamental analysis?

It is the study of economic information, central-bank policy and market expectations that can influence asset prices.

Is the course suitable for beginners?

Yes. No previous fundamental-analysis experience is required.

Can news predict Gold prices with certainty?

No. Multiple factors influence Gold prices, and market reactions are uncertain.
