What is Volume Spread Analysis (VSA)?

Volume Spread Analysis is a method of reading price spread, trading volume and candle closes together to assess how buyers and sellers may be behaving. Rather than relying on volume bars alone, VSA looks at the relationship between market activity and price movement.

In this trading mentorship, Sir Sajid Khan Ghori covers Signs of Strength (SOS), Signs of Weakness (SOW), market structure and time fractals, then connects the concepts with Commitment of Traders (COT) data and Open Interest. Students practise chart analysis and learn how to assess potential setups using context and confirmation.

Volume sources differ by market. In decentralized spot Forex, the volume shown on many retail charts is tick volume, not a complete exchange-wide count. VSA signals are not guarantees, and trades should always be planned with suitable risk management.
