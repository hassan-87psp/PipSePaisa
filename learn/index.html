Build Your Forex Foundation in the Right Order

New traders often try to learn entries before they understand the market they are entering. A stronger starting point is to learn how currency pairs are quoted, how trading sessions work, what economic news can change market expectations and how position size affects risk.

The guides below are written as practical foundations rather than trade calls. Start with the Forex overview and currency-pair guide, then study risk management before moving to more detailed technical or fundamental-analysis material. The economic-news guide is useful for understanding why gold and currencies can move sharply around major data releases and central-bank events.

These public guides connect with the wider PipSePaisa learning structure. When you are ready for a more guided path, visit the Forex Courses page or review the mentor profiles to understand the technical and fundamental teaching areas available on the platform.

As you study, focus on understanding the reason behind each concept rather than memorizing isolated rules. A clear foundation makes advanced lessons easier to evaluate and apply responsibly.
