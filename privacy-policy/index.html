PipSePaisa Privacy Policy

Learn how PipSePaisa collects, uses and protects account, course, website, analytics, notification and communication data across its Forex platform.

Last updated: 6 October 2026
This Privacy Policy explains the types of personal and technical information PipSePaisa may process when you use our website, account services, Forex courses, trading tools, VIP features and support channels.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use PipSePaisa, we may collect information such as your name, email address, WhatsApp or phone number, account details, course enrollment information, referral or campaign information, payment-verification references and communications with our support team.

We may also receive technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, website interactions, approximate technical location signals supplied by service providers, referral source and advertising or campaign parameters.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to create and secure accounts, provide course or VIP access, process enrollment, verify payments, answer support requests, deliver requested notifications, maintain platform security, understand website performance and improve PipSePaisa products and educational services.

3. Account Authentication & Security

Authentication, database and account-security functions may be provided using reputable third-party technology services. PipSePaisa does not intentionally store user passwords as readable plain text. Users should keep credentials private and contact support if they believe an account has been compromised.

4. Analytics, Cookies & Browser Storage

PipSePaisa may use cookies, local browser storage, analytics and similar technologies to understand traffic, remember preferences, support login or attribution flows, measure website performance and improve the user experience. Analytics may include information about pages viewed, device type, browser information and general interaction data.

5. Notifications & Communications

Where a user enables or requests notifications, PipSePaisa may use notification services to deliver account, course, signal, TP/SL or platform updates. Optional marketing communications should not be treated as a condition of creating an account unless clearly stated for a specific service, and users may stop optional promotional communications where an opt-out is available.

6. Payment Verification

PipSePaisa may process information reasonably required to verify a payment, including transaction references or payment confirmation. Users should not provide sensitive banking passwords, PINs or unrelated credentials. Payment providers or banks may separately process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Third-Party Technology & Service Providers

PipSePaisa may use third-party services for authentication, database hosting, analytics, notifications, website hosting, payment verification and other functionality. These providers may process limited information needed to provide their services and are governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

8. Brokers & External Links

The website may contain links to Forex brokers and other external websites. Once you leave PipSePaisa, the external provider's privacy practices apply. PipSePaisa does not control the privacy policies, account security or data handling of independent third-party platforms.

9. Referral & Attribution Data

When users arrive through a referral, advertising, campaign or broker-related link, PipSePaisa may retain relevant attribution parameters so registrations, enrollments, support routing or partner activity can be correctly recorded and managed.

10. Data Retention

We may retain account and service information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, support legitimate operational requirements and meet applicable obligations. Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted, de-identified or anonymized where appropriate.

11. Data Sharing

PipSePaisa does not need to sell personal information in order to operate the platform. Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to deliver website, account, support, analytics, notification or payment-verification functionality, or where disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect security and legal rights.

12. User Privacy Requests

Users may contact PipSePaisa regarding personal information associated with their account, including requests to review or correct inaccurate information. We may need to verify identity before making account-level changes or releasing personal information.

13. Children's Privacy

PipSePaisa is intended for users who are legally permitted to use financial education and related online services in their jurisdiction. We do not intentionally seek to collect personal information from children where such collection is not permitted.

14. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and website information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also use strong passwords, protect their devices and avoid sharing authentication credentials.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when services, technology, operational requirements or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

16. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, use the official Contact & Support page. You can also review our Terms & Conditions and Forex Trading Risk Disclaimer.
