NFP Outlook: Gold Bias Remains Slightly Bullish

PipSePaisa • Forex Education & Research

Today’s Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) report is one of the most important economic releases for Gold and the US Dollar. The current market forecast is around 80K, while the previous reading was 57K. Based on recent US labour-market indicators, our expected NFP range is approximately 78K–82K. Recent data shows that hiring momentum in the US has slowed, but the labour market has not collapsed. ADP employment data was relatively weak, while job openings also showed some softness. At the same time, jobless claims and layoffs remain controlled, which suggests that companies are not aggressively cutting workers. Because of this mixed picture, the most likely scenario is a moderate NFP reading rather than an extremely strong or extremely weak result. Our NFP Expectation Forecast: 80K Previous: 57K Expected Actual: 78K–82K Possible Impact on Gold If NFP comes below expectations, the US Dollar may weaken and Gold could receive buying support. If NFP comes well above 100K, the Dollar could strengthen and Gold may face selling pressure. If the result remains around 75K–90K, the reaction may be limited and traders may focus more on the unemployment rate, wage growth, previous-month revisions and US Treasury yields. Current Gold Bias Our current pre-news bias is slightly bullish for Gold, mainly because US hiring momentum appears softer than earlier in the year. However, the actual market reaction will depend on the complete employment report, not only the headline NFP figure.
