Forex Spread Explained: Bid, Ask, Variable Spreads and Trading Costs

PipSePaisa • Forex Education & Research

INTRODUCTION Spread is the difference between the broker's ask price and bid price. A buyer generally transacts at ask and a seller at bid. Understanding the spread prevents confusion when trades initially show a small loss even though the market price appears unchanged. 1. BID AND ASK EXAMPLE Suppose EUR/USD has bid 1.10000 and ask 1.10012. The spread is 0.00012, or 1.2 pips. A trader who immediately bought at ask and closed at bid would lose approximately the spread before commission, assuming no movement and no other fees. 2. FIXED VS VARIABLE Some accounts advertise approximately fixed spreads, often with restrictions; many have floating spreads. Variable spread can tighten in liquid periods and widen near market opens, news, rollover or sudden uncertainty. Broker quotes are also influenced by liquidity providers and execution models. 3. SPREAD IN SHORT-TERM STRATEGIES A 1.5-pip spread consumes a substantial fraction of a scalper's 5-pip target, but a smaller fraction of a swing trade targeting 150 pips. Frequent round trips accumulate costs. Compare total costs—including commissions and financing—rather than selecting accounts by quoted spread alone. 4. SPREAD AND STOP EXECUTION On platforms showing a bid chart, a buy may execute at ask while some exits and sell-side levels reference bid. Chart visualization and order execution can therefore seem different. Check platform definitions and whether stops trigger from bid or ask for the instrument. 5. MARKET VOLATILITY High-impact NFP or CPI data can make spreads wide exactly when large candles attract attention. Slippage and insufficient depth may increase actual execution costs. A published 'from 0.0 pips' quote is not a promise of zero total trading costs. 6. HOW TO COMPARE BROKERS Record average spread during your chosen trading session, commission per round turn, overnight financing, execution quality and withdrawal policies. Test on demo and small real-size conditions where appropriate; demo fills may be more favorable than live ones. FAQ Is spread the broker's only fee? No. Can spread be zero? It may be near zero temporarily on some accounts while commissions still apply. Is low spread always best? Not without evaluating execution. BID, ASK AND THE QUOTED PRICE Suppose EUR/USD shows Bid 1.10000 and Ask 1.10015. Spread is 0.00015, or 1.5 pips. A market Buy typically enters at Ask, while closing that long position commonly transacts on Bid. This is why a newly opened Buy can appear slightly negative even without a directional market move. SPREAD COST RELATIVE TO THE TARGET A 1.5-pip spread is a large fraction of a 5-pip scalping target but a smaller fraction of a 100-pip swing target. A strategy placing many trades may accumulate substantial round-trip costs. Compare net expectancy including commission, spread and swap rather than measuring raw chart moves only. FLOATING SPREAD AND LIQUIDITY During active liquid sessions, major-pair spread can sometimes narrow. Around rollover, weekend market reopening, CPI, NFP or unexpected events, spreads may widen. A broker advertising 'from zero spread' may charge commissions and does not promise zero spread every moment. WHY YOUR BUY ORDER DOESN'T MATCH THE CHART Some chart displays show Bid prices while Buy orders execute at Ask. A Buy Stop may therefore activate when Ask crosses its trigger even if the displayed Bid candle looks short of the level. Stop and pending-order rules vary by instrument and platform. Inspect quotes and contract specifications before assuming a platform error. SPREAD AND STOP LOSS If your strategy uses a small structural Stop Loss, ordinary spread expansion can consume much of the available distance or trigger exits earlier than expected. Do not solve this by arbitrarily removing protection. Either improve the setup conditions, widen the stop with smaller volume, or skip. COMPARING ACCOUNTS Record effective total trading cost in the session you intend to trade: average spread, commission per round turn, rollover financing and slippage. A lower displayed spread is not always better if execution is poor. FAQ Can spreads be negative? Quoting and execution conventions vary; retail accounts usually see nonnegative effective spread. Is zero spread a free trade? Not necessarily. Should beginners trade at NFP release because volatility is high? Not without recognizing wider execution risk. WHY THERE ARE TWO PRICES A broker commonly provides a Bid, the price at which the customer may sell, and an Ask, the price at which the customer may buy. The Ask is normally above the Bid. If EUR/USD Bid is 1.10000 and Ask is 1.10016, the spread is 0.00016, or 1.6 pips. A trader cannot assume every transaction occurs at the midpoint. Spread is a genuine part of the transaction even when no explicit commission appears. WHAT "ZERO SPREAD" CAN MEAN Some accounts advertise prices starting from a near-zero spread while charging a separate commission per side or per round trip. Other accounts charge wider spread and no explicit commission. Compare the total cost under the conditions you actually trade, not a promotional minimum. A quoted spread may be tight during active hours but widen during market rollover or unexpected events. BID CHARTS AND ORDER TRIGGERS The visible candlestick series may be based on Bid prices, while Buy orders fill on Ask. Imagine a trader places a Buy Stop at 1.1050. If Ask reaches 1.1050 but the displayed Bid candle remains slightly below, the order can appear to execute before the chart touched the line. That is not automatically a software error. Check chart display settings and instrument-specific trigger conventions. A SCALPING COST EXAMPLE A strategy aims to capture a gross favorable movement of 6 pips. Suppose spread effectively costs 1.5 pips, an equivalent commission 0.8 pip and average slippage 0.3 pip. Approximate net improvement is only 3.4 pips in equivalent terms, before swap if applicable. If losers incur comparable or larger costs, apparent paper expectancy can disappear. Very small targets are particularly sensitive to execution quality. SWING TRADING AND OVERNIGHT FINANCING A trader targeting 150 pips may care less about a 1.5-pip spread than a five-pip scalper, but overnight financing can become material when a position is held for days. Financing rates can differ for long versus short positions and may include rollover conventions. Do not ignore swaps merely because the entry spread seems small. Product documentation is the correct source for broker fees. WHY SPREAD WIDENS Liquidity suppliers change quoted prices and available depth when uncertainty rises. Around CPI, NFP, major elections, market reopenings or daily rollover, the cost of immediate execution can increase. Unexpected news may create order-book gaps. Different broker liquidity arrangements can produce different quotes at the same moment. A fixed stop in pips does not imply fixed monetary transaction costs through these conditions. SPREAD AND STOP LOSS ACTIVATION The trigger price for Stop Loss can depend on position direction and trading platform conventions. A wide spread can cause a stop to activate even if the candle chart appears not to have reached the level you expected from the other side of the quote. Test on demo and read specifications for Buy and Sell exit mechanics. Keep an execution log rather than labeling every difference manipulation. COMPARING TWO ACCOUNT TYPES Suppose Account A has a typical 1.2-pip spread and no stated commission, while Account B has 0.2 pip average spread plus 0.7 pip per round-turn equivalent commission. Account B appears cheaper under those assumptions, but realistic slippage and spread during your active session may change the comparison. Also evaluate regulation, withdrawal policies and server stability. The advertised spread is just one decision factor. MARKET ORDERS VERSUS LIMIT ORDERS Market orders seek immediate execution at available prices and may suffer slippage. Limit orders state an acceptable price but might remain unfilled. In a fast breakout, a limit order can miss the move; a market order can obtain a worse fill. Neither eliminates the need for confirmed chart structure and risk calculations. Choose order type according to the strategy, not just the lowest possible spread. HOW TO RECORD TRUE TRADING COSTS For each trade save requested entry, actual fill, requested exit, actual exit, Bid/Ask spread near entry, commission, overnight financing and account-currency conversion. Calculate realized net profit and risk multiple. This tells you whether a strategy's apparent success was due to chart movement or whether costs absorbed most gains. FAQ Does Spread exist only in Forex? No, many markets have Bid/Ask spreads. Is low Spread equal to low risk? No. Do wide spreads always prove a dishonest broker? No; changing liquidity can widen spreads legitimately. Can a broker's execution still be poor despite tight advertised spreads? Yes. A SIMPLE TOTAL-COST WORKSHEET For each broker account type, record the average entry spread during your normal session, commission for the full round trip, typical slippage in ordinary conditions and swap cost if positions are held overnight. Convert the monetary commission into a pip equivalent using the position's pip value. For example, if 0.10 lot EUR/USD has $1 pip value and round-trip commission is $0.70, commission equals roughly 0.7 pip. Combining that with a 0.8-pip effective spread produces approximately 1.5 pips in direct transaction costs before slippage. Use measured rather than advertised values when testing. SWING TRADING AUR SWAP Swing Trader 150-Pip target rakhta hai to 1.5-Pip Spread relative share small ho sakta hai. Same moment do brokers ke Bid/Ask thore different nazar aa sakte hain. Har wide spread ko immediate manipulation kehna inaccurate hai. Dono types mein entry confirmation aur Money Risk ki zaroorat same rehti hai. Order Type ko strategy ke mutabiq select karein.
