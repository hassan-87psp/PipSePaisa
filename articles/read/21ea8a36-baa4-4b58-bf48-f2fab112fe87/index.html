How to Use MetaTrader 5: Charts, Orders, Stop Loss and Trade History

PipSePaisa • Forex Education & Research

INTRODUCTION MetaTrader 5 (MT5) is a trading and market-analysis platform offered through supporting brokers. It provides charts, multiple order types, indicators, account history and algorithmic-trading capabilities. Learning platform mechanics on demo reduces preventable operational mistakes, but the interface does not determine whether a strategy is profitable. 1. ACCOUNT AND SERVER Install MT5 from an appropriate source and connect using the account login and broker server details. Confirm that the displayed server is correct and that you are on demo or live before placing orders. Be careful with passwords and avoid untrusted files marketed as trading robots. 2. MARKET WATCH AND SYMBOL SPECIFICATIONS Find EURUSD, XAUUSD or another broker-listed symbol in Market Watch. Open symbol specifications to inspect contract size, volume steps, tick size, tick value, trading hours and margin information. These settings matter because Gold and currency pairs do not necessarily share lot conventions. 3. CHART SETUP Select your timeframe, for example H1 for context and M5 for execution. Add horizontal price zones, trendlines and relevant indicators. Keep the chart readable. Save a template if you repeatedly use the same indicators, but remember indicators summarize price rather than predict it. 4. MARKET AND PENDING ORDERS A market order aims to execute at available prices immediately, subject to conditions. Buy Limit is set below the current market to seek a pullback entry; Sell Limit above. Buy Stop above the market and Sell Stop below are commonly used for breakout-style entries. Stop-Limit variants have additional behavior and may not fill. 5. SET AND MODIFY RISK Before confirming an order, check symbol, trade direction, lot size and SL/TP. Understand whether the order ticket shows points rather than pips. MT5 allows stop and target modification subject to broker distance restrictions; don't remove protection merely to avoid realizing a loss. 6. REVIEW THE HISTORY TAB Read closed trade entry/exit, commissions, swaps and total result. Export history for journal analysis. A profitable-looking chart move can still produce disappointing net results after costs. 7. PLATFORM SAFETY Check your internet connection, trading permissions and market hours. Maintain account security, use two-factor protection where your broker supports it and never send login credentials to a supposed account manager. FAQ Is MT5 a broker? No; it is a platform connected to a broker. Does installing an EA guarantee profits? No. Can MT5 show demo and live accounts? Yes, depending on the provider. MT5 ACCOUNT AND MARKET WATCH MetaTrader 5 is a platform connected to a broker rather than a broker itself. After installing it, connect to the correct trading server and verify whether you are logged into Demo or Live. Market Watch shows available instrument symbols, but each broker may use suffixes and custom contract rules. SYMBOL SPECIFICATION Before trading EUR/USD or XAUUSD open Symbol Specification to review contract size, minimum lot, lot step, tick size, tick value, allowed trading hours and margin parameters. Many errors begin when someone assumes a Gold contract behaves just like a standard currency pair. Broker stop-level distance rules can also restrict placing SL/TP too close. CHART WORKFLOW Choose H4/H1 for higher-timeframe structure and M15/M5 for setup or entry timing. Mark clear Support/Resistance, Supply/Demand and swing highs/lows. Save a clean template rather than covering the chart with dozens of indicators. Ensure the chart's visible Bid/Ask information matches the order you're planning. MARKET ORDER AND PENDING ORDER Market orders aim to trade immediately at available prices. Buy Limit normally sits below current price for a pullback; Sell Limit above for a rally. Buy Stop normally sits above price for a breakout; Sell Stop below. Stop-Limit orders combine trigger and price restrictions and may fail to fill. Always check the ticket's exact type before submission. SL/TP AND RISK CALCULATION Set the entry thesis and structural invalidation first. Determine monetary risk from Equity; convert it through distance and symbol tick value into permitted volume. Use MT5 to double-check the order's lot size and stops. A trader should not rely solely on the margin requirement shown in the terminal. HOW TO REVIEW A CLOSED TRADE Open History to inspect opening/closing prices, commissions, swaps and final result. Take a screenshot of the original chart and note what confirmation appeared on M5. Comparing expected fill with actual execution helps identify whether a strategy is vulnerable to spread or slippage. SECURITY AND FAQ Never share your broker password or install an unknown EA promising guaranteed returns. Check internet connection and whether the market is open. Is MT5 a source of market predictions? No. Can trading robots make mistakes? Yes; their logic, sizing and execution require testing. MT5 IS A PLATFORM, NOT THE BROKER MetaTrader 5 provides charts, order management, market data, account monitoring and tools for automated systems. It is connected to a trading account supplied by a broker. Different brokers can offer different symbols, lot conventions, liquidity arrangements and fees through the same-looking terminal. Before trading, verify the correct broker server, account number, demo/live status and account currency. MARKET WATCH AND SYMBOL SELECTION Market Watch lists the symbols available under your specific account. Some instruments carry suffixes, and the same-looking XAUUSD contract may have different minimum volume or tick value from another broker's Gold symbol. Open the Specification page and record Contract Size, Volume Min/Max/Step, Tick Size, Tick Value, trading hours and margin parameters. These details determine actual P&L calculations. CHART TIMEFRAMES AND BASIC NAVIGATION H4 helps view major market structure, H1 can display closer Supply/Demand and Support/Resistance, and M15/M5 can refine setup observation. Zooming into M1 does not by itself reveal a stronger signal. Set a readable candle theme, show the Bid/Ask lines if needed and avoid filling the chart with dozens of overlapping indicators. Save your preferred chart setup as a reusable template where the platform permits it. MARKET ORDERS A market Buy seeks immediate execution at the available Ask quote, while a market Sell generally transacts at Bid. The executed price can differ from the last chart label due to spread and slippage. Before submitting, check current spread, volume, direction and whether the broker has any execution restrictions. Market orders are tools for execution, not proof that an indicator signal is valid. PENDING ORDER TYPES Buy Limit typically seeks a lower pullback entry, while Sell Limit is generally placed above market for a rally entry. Buy Stop seeks an upward trigger and Sell Stop a downward trigger. Stop-Limit variants combine activation with a price restriction and may remain unfilled. Pending orders can trigger without a completed M5 confirmation, so they must match the actual entry logic. If a strategy requires candle close, an alert and manual review may fit better. STOP LOSS AND TAKE PROFIT In the order ticket enter actual Stop Loss and Take Profit prices, not just arbitrary point distances. Check that a Buy stop is on the valid side of entry and that a Sell stop is similarly valid. Minimum stop distances, execution policy and spreads are symbol-specific. Decide structural invalidation on the chart first, then calculate volume from acceptable monetary risk. A valid MT5 order can still be an excessively risky trade. ONE FULL DEMO EXERCISE Select EUR/USD on Demo and suppose the trading idea is a bullish retest. Mark a hypothetical Entry at 1.1000, Stop Loss at 1.0975 and Target at 1.1050. The planned price risk is 25 pips and possible reward 50 pips. If account equity is $1,000 with an illustrative $5 risk budget, the desired pip value is $0.20; on a typical USD account this suggests about 0.02 standard lot. Verify the broker's allowed volume and account-currency calculation. THE TRADE TAB AND EQUITY After opening a simulated position, inspect current Profit/Loss, Balance, Equity, Used Margin, Free Margin and Margin Level. Floating profit can change before a position is closed; Balance may remain unchanged while Equity moves. Learn to monitor drawdown in Equity instead of assuming the account is safe because closed Balance looks intact. MODIFYING AND CLOSING POSITIONS Practice moving a Take Profit or Stop Loss under a defined rule and closing a demo position manually. Keep a record of why the order changed. A sudden decision to remove SL after price moves against the trade is not disciplined risk management. Understand how partial closures affect remaining volume and average realized outcome. Some broker platforms offer different position accounting modes, so verify behavior on the account. HISTORY AND JOURNAL EXPORT The History section can reveal filled prices, timestamps, commissions, swaps and realized P&L. Use it alongside pre-entry screenshots to compare the planned setup with actual execution. A trade showing a favorable chart movement may have a smaller net result after costs. Exporting history to a spreadsheet can help calculate net win rate, average R, drawdown and recurring operational errors. EXPERT ADVISORS AND INDICATORS MT5 supports custom indicators and Expert Advisors built for automation, but installed files can contain faulty or misleading logic. A backtest can look excellent because of overfitting or unrealistically favorable fills. Inspect inputs such as Lot, Stop Loss, Risk Percent, symbol precision and account-locking conditions before using an EA. Run strategy tests and demo validation; code running without error does not establish profitability. PLATFORM SECURITY Obtain software and updates from legitimate sources, maintain credential security and enable account protections supported by the broker. Never send passwords or one-time verification codes to an unknown signal provider. Be careful with files advertising guaranteed returns, account recovery or automated withdrawals. Technical convenience is not a substitute for independent risk assessment. TROUBLESHOOTING ORDERS THAT DO NOT EXECUTE Check whether the market is open, whether trading permissions are enabled, whether available margin is sufficient, and whether the symbol allows the requested volume. Inspect error messages about invalid stops, price changes or trading disabled. A Buy Limit above the market may be rejected because it is the wrong order type for that price. Do not repeatedly click Submit without understanding the rejection. FAQ Is MT5 itself a regulated broker? No. Does an indicator automatically place trades? Generally not unless additional automated logic is involved. Can a pending order fill without a confirmation candle? Yes. Do lot and tick sizes differ by broker? They can. Can a demo fill differ from live execution? Yes. CONCLUSION Practice opening, modifying and closing demo orders correctly before using live capital.
