What Are Pips in Forex? Pip Value, Pipettes and Worked Examples

PipSePaisa • Forex Education & Research

INTRODUCTION A pip is a conventional minimum quoted unit for measuring price changes in many forex pairs. For most non-JPY currency pairs one pip is 0.0001; for many JPY pairs it is 0.01. Brokers may display an extra decimal called a pipette, equal to one-tenth of a pip. The contract's actual tick size and pip convention must be checked. 1. HOW TO COUNT PIPS If EUR/USD moves from 1.1000 to 1.1050, the difference is 0.0050, or 50 pips. If USD/JPY moves from 150.20 to 150.85, the difference is 0.65, or 65 pips. A five-decimal EUR/USD quote such as 1.10005 contains a fractional pip in the final digit, not a whole pip. 2. PIP VALUE IS NOT ALWAYS $10 For a standard 100,000-unit EUR/USD position, a 0.0001 move equals approximately $10 when the quote currency is USD. A 10,000-unit mini lot corresponds to about $1 per pip and a 1,000-unit micro lot to about $0.10 per pip. Those examples depend on contract size and account denomination. 3. ACCOUNT CURRENCY MATTERS With USD/JPY, each pip in a 100,000-USD position is 1,000 JPY because 100,000 × 0.01 = 1,000. If the account is in USD, the JPY amount must be converted at the prevailing exchange rate. For crosses such as EUR/GBP and a USD account, additional currency conversion may be needed. 4. PIPS, SPREAD AND NET RESULT A 1.5-pip spread means a position generally starts at an immediate transaction-cost disadvantage, before commissions or financing. A gross 30-pip favorable price move does not necessarily equal a 30-pip net profit after trading costs. Stop orders may fill at worse prices in volatile markets. 5. POSITION SIZING EXAMPLE A trader plans $15 of risk with a 25-pip stop. Ignoring extra costs, the required pip value is $15 ÷ 25 = $0.60 per pip. For a typical EUR/USD USD-denominated account, that suggests about 0.06 standard lot, but broker specifications and rounding must be verified. 6. SPECIAL CASE: GOLD AND CRYPTO Gold and crypto pip or point conventions vary between platforms and brokers. Some trading communities use custom 'pip' conventions for XAUUSD. Never transfer the 0.0001 forex definition blindly to XAUUSD; use the contract specification and a clearly stated point-to-pip rule. FAQ Is a pip the same as a point? Not always. Can a trader gain pips yet lose money? Costs, position sizing and currency conversion affect monetary results. EXACT PIP EXAMPLES EUR/USD moving from 1.08620 to 1.09270 changes by 0.00650, which is 65 pips using 0.0001 per pip. USD/JPY falling from 151.80 to 150.95 changes by 0.85, or 85 pips using 0.01 per pip. Brokers may quote one extra decimal, called a pipette: the last digit on a five-decimal EUR/USD price is typically one-tenth of a pip. PRICE MOVEMENT AND MONEY ARE DIFFERENT The pip count describes market movement; position size controls monetary exposure. A typical 100,000-unit EUR/USD position has approximately $10 per pip in a USD account; a 0.10 lot position around $1 and a 0.01 lot position around $0.10. Contract details must be verified. Two people can capture the same 50 pips yet have vastly different account results. USD/JPY CURRENCY CONVERSION With 100,000 units of USD/JPY, one 0.01 JPY move corresponds to 1,000 JPY. At an illustrative conversion of 150 JPY per USD, this is about $6.67 per pip. Unlike a typical USD-quoted EUR/USD lot, a standard USD/JPY position does not have a universally fixed $10 pip value in USD. RISK CALCULATOR EXAMPLE Suppose account equity is $500 and a hypothetical risk allowance is 1%, or $5. The chart requires 25 pips between entry and valid Stop Loss. Divide $5 by 25: maximum planned pip value is $0.20, before transaction costs. On a typical EUR/USD USD account this points toward 0.02 standard lot. If broker minimum position exceeds that risk budget, skip the trade. XAUUSD POINTS AND PIPS Gold terminology varies across brokers and trading communities. Some educational conventions describe 1,000 Gold points as 100 pips, but instrument decimal precision and tick value must be explicitly checked. Never apply EUR/USD's 0.0001 pip rule to Gold. In MT5 look up contract size, tick size and tick value under Symbol Specification. SPREAD AND REALIZED OUTCOME A gross 20-pip favorable move is not automatically a net 20-pip gain. Spread, commission, slippage and currency conversion affect the result. Compare net R multiples and money risk rather than celebrating a large gross pip total alone. FAQ Is a pip a point? Not necessarily. Is every standard lot $10 per pip? No. Can I lose money with a positive pip count? Costs and differing size can distort comparisons. UNDERSTANDING QUOTATION PRECISION In common retail currency quotes, 1 pip on EUR/USD is 0.0001 and on USD/JPY is 0.01. A fifth decimal for EUR/USD or third decimal for JPY pairs frequently represents one-tenth of a pip, or a pipette. The last displayed digit is not automatically a whole pip. Trading terminals may also describe the minimum price increment as a point or tick. These terms should be checked against the symbol specification. A FULL EUR/USD EXAMPLE Suppose EUR/USD moves from 1.08540 to 1.08875. Subtract to obtain 0.00335. Divide by 0.0001 to find 33.5 pips. If a position held 0.10 standard lot, the gross pip value may be around $1 per pip in a USD account, making the favorable gross price movement about $33.50 before spread, commissions and financing. The calculation changes for a different contract or account currency. A FULL JPY-PAIR EXAMPLE Imagine USD/JPY falls from 152.30 to 151.55. The move is 0.75 yen per US dollar, or 75 pips using the 0.01 pip convention. At 100,000 USD notional, each pip represents 1,000 JPY, not a fixed $10. The account-currency equivalent depends on the current exchange conversion. That is why pip count and profit in dollars are separate concepts. CROSS CURRENCY PAIRS For EUR/GBP, a 100,000-EUR standard contract and a 0.0001 price move have a 10-GBP pip value. If the account is denominated in USD, that 10 GBP must be converted using an appropriate GBP/USD rate. A trader comparing EUR/GBP with EUR/USD cannot use identical dollar pip assumptions without this additional step. Broker products can implement conversion and fees differently. DETERMINING LOT SIZE FROM PIP VALUE Suppose equity is $750, intended risk is 0.8%, or $6, and Stop Loss is 30 pips from entry. The maximum planned pip value before fees is $6 ÷ 30 = $0.20. On typical USD-denominated EUR/USD this corresponds to about 0.02 standard lot. But if a fee of $1 is expected, using the full $6 for price risk may exceed the total loss budget. Round volume down when necessary. SPREAD AND THE NET PIP OUTCOME A strategy may show 25 pips of favorable chart movement while the entry and exit prices are separated by Bid/Ask spread. Add explicit commissions and slippage to obtain the actual account result. Distinguish gross movement between two mid-prices from realized monetary P&L. A published performance report should disclose its convention for what counts as one pip and whether costs are included. STOP LOSS IN POINTS On a five-decimal EUR/USD quote, some platforms represent a pip as ten points. A request for a stop distance of '250 points' may correspond to 25 pips, depending on the broker's point size. The same assumption must not be carried over to Gold or Bitcoin. Before entering a position, check the actual price printed next to the Stop Loss rather than trusting the entered number of points alone. GOLD: A CUSTOM CONVENTION CAN BE VALID ONLY IF EXPLAINED PipSePaisa may communicate XAUUSD performance using the educational convention that a $10 Gold move equals 100 pips. Other platforms may call that same price movement 1,000 points or use a different convention. There is no universally interchangeable XAUUSD pip terminology across all brokers. When analyzing Gold, state both the start and end price and the convention used. Monetary P&L should always come from the contract's tick value and volume. COMMON ERRORS AND SELF-TEST Do not confuse 0.01 lot with one pip, assume a 100-pip move always pays $1,000, or treat standard lot exposure as a fixed cash deposit. Practice: EUR/USD from 1.10000 to 1.10250 is 25 pips; USD/JPY from 150.00 to 150.35 is 35 pips. If you cannot compute these independently, learn the quotation format before using leveraged positions. ONE MORE PRACTICAL RISK CHECK Before accepting a EUR/USD entry, calculate the precise price difference between entry and Stop Loss and convert it to pips using the symbol's pip convention. Then multiply by Pip Value at the selected volume to estimate monetary risk. Add estimated commission and consider slippage. Repeat the same exercise for a USD/JPY position and an XAUUSD position; the results should differ because contract and quotation rules differ. If the platform offers a profit calculator, compare its estimates with your manual method rather than treating the calculator as infallible. Record the price levels in performance reports so that other readers can interpret a pip count accurately. CONCLUSION Always separate price movement (pips), position size (units/lots), pip value (account currency) and net P&L (after costs).
