PipSePaisa V18 — Live Test Checklist

Run these tests only after Query 58, Edge Function redeploy, Auth email template update and website upload.

1. SIGNUP
- Create a fresh account.
- The signup modal must immediately stay on “Check Your Email”.
- It must not auto-close or switch to Login.
- Verification email should arrive.

2. EMAIL VERIFICATION
- Click the verification link.
- email-verified.html must open.
- Dashboard must NOT auto-open.
- Click Continue to Sign In and log in manually.
- Free Access PIN instruction email should arrive.

3. PIN ACCESS
- In Admin Panel > Users, confirm the user has a unique PIN and deadline.
- In Admin Panel > Settings, set a short test timer such as 1 minute and click Apply Timer to All Pending Users.
- In the user Settings page, confirm Name, Email, WhatsApp, PIN status, deadline and PIN input are visible.
- After expiry, Signals, Charts, Articles and Journal should remain visible but be blurred/locked.
- Clicking locked content must show the “totally free” WhatsApp/PIN popup.
- Enter the correct PIN in Settings and confirm protected content unlocks.

4. FREE COURSE
- Enroll in the Basic course.
- Success should appear immediately without a long visible “Submitting…” state.
- Free enrollment email should arrive.

5. PAID COURSE / RECEIPT
- Test JPG/JPEG, PNG and PDF receipt uploads.
- A file larger than 5 MB or an unsupported type must show a clear error.
- Payment submission success should appear immediately.
- “Payment Received — Under Review” email should arrive.

6. ADMIN PAYMENT ACTIONS
- Approve payment: course unlocks and approval email arrives.
- Edit payment details/status after approval.
- Revoke approval: course locks again and revocation email arrives.
- Resubmit after rejected/revoked status.

7. COURSE ADMIN EDITOR
- Upload a direct 16:9 thumbnail (recommended 1200 × 675).
- Edit title, full/short/extra descriptions, price, badge, labels, access-card features, requirements, audience, six learning outcomes, six achievement outcomes, modules, content note, secure line, mentor text and button text.
- Confirm both free and paid course cards use clean 16:9 thumbnails without side bars or overflow.
