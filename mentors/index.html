Learn With Technical and Fundamental Analysis Mentors

PipSePaisa separates key areas of Forex education so learners can understand both how price behaves and why broader market conditions can change. Technical-analysis learning covers chart structure, candlesticks, trading sessions, liquidity, volume-based concepts and risk-controlled execution. Fundamental-analysis learning focuses on economic data, central-bank decisions, inflation, employment reports and the drivers that can influence currencies and gold.

Use the mentor profiles to review each instructor’s teaching focus, then visit the Courses page for currently available learning programs. Beginners can also start with the free Forex learning guides before joining a structured course.
