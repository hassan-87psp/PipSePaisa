This includes major economic releases, inflation, employment data, central-bank decisions, interest-rate expectations and broader market sentiment.

Understanding economic data

Fundamental analysis is more than reading whether a number is higher or lower than the previous value. Markets often react to the difference between the actual release and the expected forecast, as well as revisions and details inside the report. Learners therefore need to understand both the data and the market expectations that existed before the release.

Central-bank communication can also change the market’s view of future interest rates. Those expectations may affect currencies, bond yields and gold. Because different forces can operate at the same time, fundamental analysis is best used as a structured framework rather than a guarantee that price must move in one direction.

Gold and Forex market drivers

Gold can react to the US dollar, real yields, inflation expectations, risk sentiment and major geopolitical or economic developments. Forex pairs are influenced by the relative outlook for both currencies in the pair. Learners can use fundamental context together with technical levels and risk controls to build a more complete decision process.
