Sajid Khan Ghori — Technical Analysis Education

Sajid Khan Ghori is presented on PipSePaisa as a Forex technical-analysis instructor. His teaching focus is built around reading price in a structured way rather than depending on a single indicator or one isolated candle. Learners are introduced to market structure, price action, candlestick behaviour, Volume Spread Analysis, trading sessions, liquidity and risk management as connected parts of a trading process.

What learners study

The technical-analysis path is designed to help students understand where price is trading, what the recent structure is showing and what kind of confirmation may be required before an entry is considered. Candlestick analysis is used as context and confirmation, while session timing and liquidity concepts help explain why the same setup can behave differently at different times of the trading day.

Risk management is treated as part of execution rather than a separate topic. Position size, stop placement, risk-to-reward and trade review matter because a valid analysis can still lead to a losing trade. The objective of structured education is therefore to improve decision quality and consistency, not to promise a perfect win rate.
