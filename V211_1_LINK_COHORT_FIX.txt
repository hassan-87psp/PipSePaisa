PipSePaisa V211.1 — Strict Link Cohort Analytics

Fixes:
- Enrolled Users can never exceed Signups.
- Signups are unique people from the exact tracked link.
- Enrolled Users are only those same signup people who later enrolled on the same link.
- Repeated enrollments / multiple courses do not inflate Enrolled Users.
- Conversion = Enrolled Users / Signups, capped at 100%.
- Existing click and raw tracking-event history is preserved.

Deploy:
1. Run 86_V211_1_LINK_COHORT_ANALYTICS.sql in Supabase SQL Editor.
2. Upload admin-panel.html and link-manager-v211-1.js from this patch.
